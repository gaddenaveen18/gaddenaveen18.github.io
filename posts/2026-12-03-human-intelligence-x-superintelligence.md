Imagine two people facing the same problem. One has every AI tool. The other has only experience and curiosity.
Five years later, who has solved more important problems?

Is problem solving an art, imagination or analysis? In truth, it is all three at once.

First, know the kind of problem:
→ Clear: follow what works.
→ Layered: study, then decide.
→ Living: try small, learn fast.
→ Tangled: align first, then solve.

Then look through two lenses:
👤 The human view: people, process, behavior, time, risk, system.
🤖 The AI view: patterns, scale, speed, memory.

Neither is complete alone. Analysis without judgment can be precise and still wrong. Judgment without analysis can be wise and still miss what is hidden.

Humans see what matters. AI sees what is hidden.
Human insight × AI analysis = universal solutions.

💭 Worth a thought: what kind of problem are you really solving?
