Growth and giving: separate, the same, or two sides of one loop?

Imagine you teach someone everything you know. Years later, they are teaching you. Who gave, and who grew?

**♾️ The Infinity Loop: 7 steps to grow through giving**
① Self-awareness: notice what you have that someone else hasn't earned yet.
② Step outside routine: real growth happens outside your comfort zone.
③ Contribute: give a specific, usable skill, not vague goodwill.
④ Empathy through exposure: listen more than you advise.
⑤ Relationships: shared purpose creates bonds that outlast the project.
⑥ Reflect: growth that isn't examined tends to fade.
⑦ Reinvest: what you just learned becomes your next thing to give.

In the superintelligence era, answers are everywhere. What stays rare is what only people can give: time, skill, empathy and belief in someone.

Run the loop enough times, and giving back stops being something you schedule. It becomes how you grow.

Give back. Learn back. Grow within. Live on.

💭 Worth a thought: where are you in the loop today?
