Imagine you fix a problem perfectly. Six months later, it is back.
Was it ever really solved?

Most problems we face are not new. They are old problems that came back: fixed, but never really solved.

Think of an iceberg:
→ The visible issue: what we fix.
→ The hidden cause: why it happens.
→ The system design: why it can happen at all.
Most fixes stop at the tip. Lasting solutions reach the bottom.

**🔁 The 3-Step Pattern**
① Name it: state the friction precisely, what is happening and why.
② Weigh it: quick fix, strategic pivot or structural rebuild.
③ Build it in: make the fix hold without heroics.

It works beyond work too:
→ Math: memorize the answer, or understand the method.
→ Fitness: a crash diet, or a daily habit.
→ Relationships: an apology, or changed behavior.

A fix ends the incident. A solution ends the problem.

💭 Worth a thought: did you solve the problem, or remove the conditions that created it?
