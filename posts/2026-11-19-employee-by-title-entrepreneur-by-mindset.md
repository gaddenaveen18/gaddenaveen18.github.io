### 💡 A job or a way of thinking?

We often think entrepreneurs are people who start companies.
But being entrepreneurial is really about how we think.

Some founders think like employees.
Some employees think like founders.

✔ Owning a business doesn't make us think like builders. Not owning one doesn't stop us.
✔ A title can't promise impact. It can't limit it either.
✔ Engineer, teacher, nurse, student or founder: the mindset is open to everyone.

### 🔄 Doing the job, or growing it?

One way to see work:
📋 Role → Tasks → Results

Another way:
🚀 Ownership → Possibilities → Value

The first keeps things running. The second moves things forward. We need both.

Our roles may be fixed.
Our mindset isn't.

### 🎯 Given or chosen?

A title is given to us.
A mindset is chosen by us.
And that choice shapes what we do with everything else.

### ⚡ What changes?

🌱 For ourselves: we keep learning and growing
🏢 For our teams: we add value beyond our job descriptions
🌍 For the world: we leave things better than we found them

### 🌟 So who shapes the future?

Not titles. People.
People who care, take ownership and create value, wherever they are.

Some of the most entrepreneurial people never leave their jobs.
And the best founders build teams where everyone thinks this way.

Worth a thought: are our titles shaping our work, or our mindsets?
