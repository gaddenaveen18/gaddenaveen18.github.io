**Augment, don't substitute.**

- ✨ **Being competitive is nice, but being accountable is efficient.**
- ✨ **Being productive is nice, but being creative is efficient.**
- ✨ **Being guided is nice, but being the example is efficient.**

There are two ways to bring AI into work. **Augmentation** puts people and AI side by side. **Substitution** swaps people out and hopes the output holds. In the short run, both can look efficient. Over time, one compounds and the other erodes.

[![True Efficiency in the AI Era](/assets/posts/true-efficiency-in-the-ai-era.jpg)](/assets/posts/true-efficiency-in-the-ai-era.jpg)

## Three differences that decide the outcome

### 1. Accountability
- **Augmentation, auditing outputs:** "We make sure the results are right."
- **Substitution, ignoring outputs:** "Who checks the results?"

### 2. Creativity
- **Augmentation, enhancing human ingenuity:** "We innovate and solve problems."
- **Substitution, limiting human input:** "We just do what we're told."

### 3. Example
- **Augmentation, guiding AI with high-quality examples:** "We lead by example."
- **Substitution, lack of proper guidance:** "We just follow the program."

### Where each path leads
- **Augmentation:** responsible, creative and inspiring, which leads to **growth**.
- **Substitution:** disconnected, limited and declining, which leads to **decline**.

## Putting it into practice

1. **Audit the outputs.** Review what AI produces before it ships. Accountability stays with people.
2. **Amplify ingenuity.** Use AI to take on routine work, and spend the time saved on new ideas.
3. **Set the example.** Give AI high-quality examples to follow, and be the example for your team.

## The key principle

**Efficiency in the AI era comes from auditing outputs (accountability), enhancing human ingenuity (creativity) and guiding AI with high-quality examples (example).**

## Try it with your team

- Who reviews your AI's output before it reaches a customer?
- What did your team do with the last hour AI saved them?
- Are you guiding your AI tools, or are they guiding you?
