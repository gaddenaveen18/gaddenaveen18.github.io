A perfect answer to the wrong question is still the wrong outcome. Most thinking is a line, and lines end. The Question Loop is a cycle instead: seven stages for leadership, AI, sales, engineering and life. Not a line. A loop.

## The seven stages

1. **Question**: What is really needed?
2. **Understanding**: Read it the way they mean it.
3. **Options**: Every path, not just the obvious.
4. **Ownership**: Us, someone else, or AI?
5. **Action**: Do, test, refine.
6. **Outcome**: Real value for the one who asked.
7. **Better Question**: Learn, then ask sharper.

The final stage is where learning begins. Every outcome feeds a sharper next question, so the loop keeps evolving instead of stopping at the answer.

## One loop, every arena

- **Selling to an end user:** "What problem are they trying to solve?", not "How do I sell this?"
- **Leading a team:** "What did the system make easy to get wrong?", not "Who dropped the ball?"
- **Engineering and operations:** "Why did it happen, and will the fix hold?", not "How do I close this?"
- **Invoking a program or AI:** "Here's my goal, context and limits. What am I missing?", not "Give me an answer."
- **Superintelligence:** "What should we want, and how will we know it's right?", not "What can it do for us?"

## Every day runs on questions

- **Start of day:** What truly matters today, and why?
- **Through the day:** What is really being asked of me here?
- **End of day:** What will I ask better tomorrow?
